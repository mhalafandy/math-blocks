# Text guard fixtures

Each `*.json` is one whole-document operation as the add-in's text guard sees it: the document before
(`before`) and after (`after`), recorded as `WordOps.Operation.RecordGuardDoc` does (every field, equation,
picture and list of symbols by its range, and Word's text between their boundaries), the ranges the operation
was asked to replace (`exclude`) and the expected verdict (`expect`: `unchanged` unless stated). `GuardTests.cs`
checks each one with `GuardText` (no Word), and that a typed word or a deleted space in the text is caught.

- `gen-*.json`: generated by `tools/make-guard-fixtures.ts` from the field codes the add-in writes.
- `rec-*.json`: recorded by the self-test in Word (its folder's `guard-fixtures/`), generic test documents only.

Rule: anything new the add-in writes into Word (fields, bookmarks, markers, tables) gets a fixture here before
it is published (docs/DEVELOPMENT.md §4).
